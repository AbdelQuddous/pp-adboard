AdBoard

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

AdBoard helps AdMob publishers view reporting data on mobile. It requests read-only access to AdMob reporting data and cannot change your ads, payments, or account configuration.

1. Who we are

AdBoard: AdMob Earnings Tracker ("AdBoard", "we", "us") is provided by AbdelQuddos. This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you use it.

2. Information the app handles

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and provide read-only access to your AdMob reporting data.
  • Display requested reports, charts, account breakdowns, currency preferences, and cached recent data.
  • Maintain the app, diagnose errors, and understand whether core flows such as sign-in and dashboard loading work.
  • Show native advertising to eligible free users.
  • Send local notifications only when you enable them in the app.

4. Google services and third parties

AdBoard uses Google services including Google Sign-In, the Google AdMob API, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics, and Google Mobile Ads. These providers process information as described in their own policies. We do not sell your AdMob reporting data or operate a separate developer-controlled backend that receives your AdMob reporting data.

When you authorize AdBoard to access your Google data, our use of that data is limited to providing and improving AdBoard's user-facing features, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

5. Local storage and security

AdBoard keeps recent reporting data and preferences locally on your device to support faster loading and offline viewing. This cached reporting data is stored in the app's local storage. Access tokens are stored separately using platform secure storage. Data sent between the app and Google services is transmitted over HTTPS.

No security method is perfect. Please keep your device protected and use the in-app controls to sign out or clear local data when needed.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Demo Mode: lets you explore sample data without accessing an AdMob account. App usage analytics may still be collected as described above.
  • Sign out: signs you out and removes locally stored app data.
  • Clear local data: available in Profile > Privacy & Data. It removes cached reporting data, stored tokens, preferences, and notification schedules from the app.
  • Revoke access: you may revoke AdBoard's access through your Google Account permissions.
  • Notifications: you can enable or disable notifications in the app settings.

7. Data retention

Locally cached reporting data and preferences remain on your device until you sign out, clear local data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Access tokens expire and may be refreshed while you remain signed in. Google and Firebase may retain information according to their respective policies.

8. Children

AdBoard is intended for app publishers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International processing

Google services may process information in countries where Google or its service providers operate. Those transfers are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app.
