Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Your AdMob reports stay private. MonetBoard signs in through Google's official OAuth flow and requests read-only access to your AdMob reports. Reports travel directly from Google to your device. They are not sent to or stored on any server operated by MonetBoard or its developer, and the developer cannot see them. MonetBoard never receives your Google password.

1. Who we are

MonetBoard ("MonetBoard", "we", "us"), previously named AdBoard, is a mobile app for AdMob publishers provided by AbdelQuddos. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

3. What the developer can and cannot access

Can the developer see my AdMob reports?

No. MonetBoard has no developer-operated server that receives or stores your AdMob reporting data. Your device requests reports directly from Google's AdMob API, then displays and caches them locally. The developer cannot view your earnings, metrics, account details, ad-unit details, access tokens or raw API responses.

Does MonetBoard receive my Google password?

No. Sign-in and authorization take place in Google's own interface. Your password is never provided to or stored by MonetBoard or its developer.

Can MonetBoard change anything in my AdMob account?

No. MonetBoard requests only read-only AdMob reporting permissions. It cannot create, edit or delete apps or ad units, change payment details, manage ads or alter account settings. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account.

4. How we use information

  • Sign you in and provide read-only access to your AdMob reports.
  • Show your reports, comparisons, signals, breakdowns and cached recent data, using the currency and settings you choose.
  • Daily report notification: if you turn on notifications (they are off by default), the app checks once a day, in the background on your device, whether yesterday's report is available. To do this it may silently renew your Google access token and request the report from Google. The notification only says that your report is ready; it does not show earnings or account details.
  • Show ads to users of the free version.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • If you opt in to analytics, understand feature use and fix reliability problems.

5. Google services and other third parties

MonetBoard uses Google Sign-In, the Google AdMob API, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, the Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, and Google Play in-app review and in-app update. On iOS, purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy.

To show your apps' icons, the app sends the package name or bundle ID of each app in your AdMob account to public app store pages (Google Play and the Apple App Store, and as fallbacks APKPure and AppBrain). These requests come directly from your device, so those services can see your IP address and which app identifiers were looked up. No reporting data is included.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share your AdMob reporting data with anyone.

MonetBoard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide MonetBoard's user-facing features and is never used for advertising.

6. Advertising and consent

The free version shows ads from Google Mobile Ads, including native ads within the app and occasional full-screen ads between screens. Ads are not shown in demo mode, while privacy mode is on, or after you buy ad-free use.

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Account > Privacy & data > Ad choices.

7. Local storage and security

Recent reports and preferences are stored in the app's local storage so the app loads quickly and works offline. Access tokens are kept separately in platform secure storage. All communication with Google and other services uses HTTPS.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected and use the controls below to sign out or clear data when needed.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Product analytics: off by default. Turn it on or off in Account > Privacy & data.
  • Ad choices: review or change your ad consent in Account > Privacy & data.
  • Notifications: off by default. Turn the daily report notification on or off in Account > Notifications.
  • Privacy mode: hides all amounts on screen.
  • Demo mode: explore the app with sample data without connecting an AdMob account.
  • Sign out: signs you out and removes your account and report data from the device. App preferences such as notification time and consent choices are kept.
  • Clear local data and disconnect: in Account > Privacy & data. Removes all cached reports, tokens, preferences and scheduled notifications from the device.
  • Revoke access: remove MonetBoard's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

9. Data retention

Cached reports and preferences stay on your device until you sign out, clear local data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Access tokens expire and are renewed only while you remain signed in. Google and Firebase retain information according to their own policies.

10. Children

MonetBoard is designed for app publishers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International processing

Google and the other services listed above may process information in countries other than your own. Those transfers are governed by each provider's terms and privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or its data practices change, and revise the effective date above. Where appropriate, we will also let you know in the app.
